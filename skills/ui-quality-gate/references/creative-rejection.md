# Creative character review for ambitious UI

Use **after** task, usability, accessibility and ordinary craft inspection, not instead of them. Scope: newly branded landing pages, expressive marketing surfaces and redesigns where the brief specifically calls for distinctiveness. A conventional design is not intrinsically defective; tie every finding to the actual brief and product.

## Reviewer protocol

View the full desktop and mobile renders **without the builder's rationale or desired verdict**. First write what the product appears to do, where its real visual proof sits and what action is available. Next inspect the comparison concepts, if the project is at Shape depth. Do not let a polished explanation substitute for a compelling artifact.

| Diagnostic | Ask and inspect | Major defect when a distinctive identity is in scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Logo swap** | Mask the logo; imagine exchanging only name, copy and accent color with an adjacent competitor. Assess layout, product representation, imagery, type relationships and interaction. | The primary visual and section organization could serve the competitor unchanged; no product-specific focal point remains |
| **Asset preservation** | Locate existing meaningful product UI, real avatars, mascots, photography or recognized marks in the final page. | Authentic signature evidence was removed in favor of unrelated abstract artwork without improved clarity or an explicit rebrand decision |
| **Product proof** | Identify what the user provides, what the product does and what they receive. Check if the demo is real, recorded, or labeled illustrative. | Generic visuals replace the promised mechanism; fictitious screenshots/proof are passed off as actual |
| **Distinct alternatives** | Compare same content and constraints. Examine narrative, composition and interaction, not just tokens. | “Alternatives” only restyle one template and the project presents that as substantial creative exploration |
| **First-viewport hierarchy** | Examine headline, artifact, trust and action at normal scale on desktop and mobile. | An immersive effect, typography or dead space delays recognition or the actual next action |
| **Character under subtraction** | Mentally remove the signature animation, gradient and decorative texture. | No recognizable editorial or product-specific voice remains, although the brief requires one |

## Findings and correction

For each failure document **location, observed relationship, visitor/brand consequence, severity, actionable correction and evidence**. A screenshot supports composition judgment, not interaction latency, user memory or proven conversion uplift. For an asset, compare with the actual available original. Do not assert the asset is missing when it was not provided or inspected.

A significant creative failure is a **major** in an ambitious brief and blocks the gate's “ready” verdict even if responsive, semantic and component checks pass. Route the correction to Vibe UI's concept/narrative decision. **Do not fix a generic composition by adding noise, gradients, shadows, rounded cards or random animations.**

If evidence is missing, mark **unverified**. If the work is an established restrained product, useful template, compliance surface or targeted repair, do not fail it merely because a competitor could use familiar components. Assess meaningful differentiation at the correct level.

## Counterexample

A page with perfect responsive buttons, balanced spacing and semantic headings, but a headline such as “AI-powered productivity reimagined,” floating generic cards, a purple wash and no product view, **fails** a differentiation-oriented landing-page brief despite likely passing many mechanical checks.

A restrained page with literal, differentiated copy, an honest product demo, real artifacts and one clear CTA **may pass** without fancy gradients, animation or nonstandard navigation. The quality requirement is product fit, not decorative novelty.

## Evidence boundaries

The logo-swap exercise is expert critique, not a validated measure of uniqueness or end-user recall. Record competing references and the actual captures used. Owner rejection reopens the applicable finding regardless of the previous assessor's judgment.
