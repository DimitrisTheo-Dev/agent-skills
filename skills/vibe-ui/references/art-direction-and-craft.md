# Art direction and coherence

Vibe UI owns the relationship between structure, type, color, materials, imagery and behavior. For
detailed decisions use [specialist orchestration](specialist-orchestration.md); keep one direction
and one shared record.

## Creative brief

State the functional promise, desired emotional register, recognizable product-specific detail and
main visual tension. For example, expressive content can sit inside quiet functional chrome. Name a
restraint that protects the idea. Avoid a direction made only of adjectives.

Use [direction selection](direction-selection.md) when a meaningful choice is open. Keep evidence
before retiring disposable code. Make reversible choices autonomously and ask when a consequential
user priority is missing.

## Signature and imagery

Identity can emerge from real product output, a distinctive diagram, editorial crop, original
illustration, meaningful data, typographic voice or authentic work photography. Do not fabricate
customers, credentials or proof. Generated imagery must not masquerade as actual work or product
output.

Choose imagery by its job: recognition, explanation, atmosphere or evidence. Reserve its dimensions,
preserve the focal subject and specify missing-asset behavior. A random thumbnail cannot rescue a
list; a meaningful preview can change how someone chooses.

Judge materials by the whole composition. Gradients, glass, texture, shadows and sharp or rounded
surfaces can all be appropriate. Explain their contribution to understanding, confidence, pleasure
or identity, then inspect readability and runtime cost. Neither sparse monochrome nor decorative
spectacle is a universal objective.

## Moment hypothesis

Distinguish an occasional beat, such as the first useful result, from everyday feel, such as direct
manipulation or less expected work. When testing a signature interaction, name the benefit, what
would confirm it and how it works with varied inputs and on repeat use. Use an accessible
alternative where needed. Prototype it in build scope when its effect could change the decision.

Character should remain in composition, type, content and ordinary interactions when the special
moment is removed. If no candidate earns its cost, improve everyday feel and state that finding. A
satisfying response can be quiet.

## Cross-concern critique

Examine the whole viewport at normal scale before details. Does the strongest region deserve
attention? Does the chosen font support the content? Are color proportions reinforcing hierarchy? Do
spacing and material express the same density and tone? Does feedback preserve the meaning of the
action?

Use focused specialists to correct the cause. Do not resolve a structure problem by adding shadows,
a terminology problem by adding an icon, or a latency problem by inventing progress. Keep supporting
controls familiar and give distinctive elements a reason.

## Standalone fallback

If specialists are unavailable, preserve the existing system and resolve the relevant roles
directly: task structure; display/body/label/numeric type; surface/text/action/status colors;
relational spacing and component geometry; immediate feedback, recovery and reduced motion. Specify
concrete choices and inspect the real screen. Local visual QA remains the fallback review; do not
claim checks that could not run.
