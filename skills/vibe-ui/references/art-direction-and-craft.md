# Art direction and coherence

Vibe UI owns the relationship between structure, type, color, materials, imagery and behavior. For detailed decisions use [specialist orchestration](specialist-orchestration.md); keep one direction and one shared record.

## Creative brief

State the functional promise, desired emotional register, recognizable product-specific detail and main visual tension. For example, expressive content can sit inside quiet functional chrome. Name a restraint that protects the idea. Avoid a direction made only of adjectives.

For Shape work, compare two or three coherent directions on identical content. Vary composition, hierarchy or interaction meaningfully. For Extend, compare only an unsettled choice. Keep evidence before retiring disposable code. Make reversible choices autonomously; ask only for consequential missing priorities.

## Signature and imagery

Identity can emerge from real product output, a distinctive diagram, editorial crop, original illustration, meaningful data, typographic voice or authentic work photography. Do not fabricate customers, credentials or proof. Generated imagery must not masquerade as actual work or product output.

Choose imagery by its job: recognition, explanation, atmosphere or evidence. Reserve its dimensions, preserve the focal subject and specify missing-asset behavior. A random thumbnail cannot rescue a list; a meaningful preview can change how someone chooses.

Judge materials by the whole composition. Gradients, glass, texture, shadows and sharp or rounded surfaces can all be appropriate. Explain their contribution to understanding, confidence, pleasure or identity, then inspect readability and runtime cost. Neither sparse monochrome nor decorative spectacle is a universal objective.

## Moment hypothesis

Distinguish an occasional beat, such as the first useful result, from everyday feel, such as direct manipulation or less expected work. Specify trigger, benefit, feeling, behavior, repeated-use cost, accessible alternative and confirming/refuting evidence. Prototype the strongest candidate in build scope. A copy toast does not become a signature moment by renaming it.

Character should remain in composition, type, content and ordinary interactions when the special moment is removed. If no candidate earns its cost, improve everyday feel and state that finding. A satisfying response can be quiet.

## Cross-concern critique

Examine the whole viewport at normal scale before details. Does the strongest region deserve attention? Does the chosen font support the content? Are color proportions reinforcing hierarchy? Do spacing and material express the same density and tone? Does feedback preserve the meaning of the action?

Use focused specialists to correct the cause. Do not resolve a structure problem by adding shadows, a terminology problem by adding an icon, or a latency problem by inventing progress. Keep supporting controls familiar and give distinctive elements a reason.

## Standalone fallback

If specialists are unavailable, preserve the existing system and resolve the relevant roles directly: task structure; display/body/label/numeric type; surface/text/action/status colors; relational spacing and component geometry; immediate feedback, recovery and reduced motion. Specify concrete choices and inspect the real screen. Local visual QA remains the fallback review; do not claim checks that could not run.
