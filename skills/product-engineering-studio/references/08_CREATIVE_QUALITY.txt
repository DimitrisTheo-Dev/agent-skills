CREATIVE QUALITY
Use when choosing a direction or assessing a substantial UI. Quality and Design covers the relevant engineering, accessibility and trust checks.

A. DEPTH AND BUDGET
Fix keeps established decisions and checks the affected behavior. Extend applies those decisions to new work, with exploration where a choice is unsettled. Shape covers a new product, identity or core journey. The main uncertainty determines what to explore. Reserve time to operate, edit and recheck the first complete flow. Respect the user's time and scope.

B. READ THE PRODUCT
Ground the direction in the user's outcome, task frequency, real content and product constraints. Use a short point of view when it would resolve choices. An experienced dispatcher needs exceptions and next actions visible across many records. That favors aligned rows, persistent keyboard context and restrained feedback for routine changes. Familiar conventions can support a specific point of view.

C. STUDY RELATIONSHIPS
Use supplied screenshots, palettes and source material as evidence. Name the relationship studied, why it matters here and how it translates: accent proportions, text/image balance, density, disclosure, or interaction continuity. Record accessible sources and distinguish observations from interpretation. Study a reference interaction live or in recorded evidence when behavior matters. Never infer motion from a still.
Gather a small relevant comparison set when tools permit. If access fails, use available evidence and record the limit. Match explicit recreation requests; otherwise interpret references for the product rather than offering a fixed style or palette menu.

D. COMPARE WHEN IT CAN CHANGE THE CHOICE
Use Vibe UI for a consequential open design decision when available. If working standalone, compare meaningfully different structures on the same task and realistic content. Test the busy or difficult case and the viewport or interaction that makes the choice consequential. Choose based on task access, comprehension, product fit and cost. One working slice can answer a feasibility or interaction question. Section B of Workflow covers learning prototypes.
Keep the reason and consequential tradeoff where the project records decisions. Use a reversible choice when the evidence is adequate. Ask when the choice depends on a missing user priority with material consequences.

E. COLOR IN CONTEXT
For each direction explain dominant surfaces, text, accent meanings, supporting status/data colors, rough proportions and the relationship to imagery or materials. Judge the palette on real screens, content and supported themes, not equal-sized swatches. A familiar palette may be correct when grounded in the product.
After selection, build semantic roles and relevant hover/pressed/selected/focus/disabled and feedback pairings. Measure contrast on computed colors. Keep the palette consistent within this product; never promote its hues, typeface or motif as a default for the next product.

F. CHARACTER AND MOMENTS
Carry a point of view through composition, typography, writing, materials and everyday interaction. Remove the signature moment mentally: the remainder must still feel considered and specific.
Seek places where the product's value becomes tangible, effortless or satisfying. A rare first result can support a richer moment. Everyday actions benefit from quick feedback, retained context and less work. Prototype a signature interaction when its effect matters to the brief. Inspect first use, repeated use and accessible alternatives as relevant. Keep predictions about long-term use distinct from observations.

G. BUILD CRAFT INTO THE FIRST FLOW
Use suitable components and tokens to build a deliberate composition, readable hierarchy and feedback into the first useful flow. Inspect designs with their actual states and input methods. Keep success tied to the authoritative outcome and preserve recoverable work. Separate platform and accessibility requirements from hypotheses about density, visual expression or timing. Test the hypotheses that affect use.

H. ASSESS FOUR WAYS
Use a reviewer who did not build the flow when tools and delegation rules permit. Supply the exact candidate, product brief, real references and evidence; withhold the builder's self-rating and desired verdict. If independent review is unavailable, do a labelled self-review and continue authorized work.
Correctness: execute behavior criteria, relevant states and failure paths. Checks support only the exercised cases.
Usability: attempt a cold-start task by the relevant inputs and assess first-look comprehension. Observe people when available; otherwise label this a reviewer walkthrough, not a user test.
Craft: inspect rendered composition, type, rhythm, color, optical alignment, surfaces, states, input and motion at representative widths, adjacent breakpoints and supported themes. Measure what can be measured. A passing checklist can still describe an ordinary product.
Character: assess a compelling composition, product specificity, distinctive care/value, repeated-use satisfaction and comparison to references. Evaluate the whole flow with the signature moment present and absent.
Use screenshots for appearance and recordings, frame strips, measurements or live interaction for temporal claims. State what the reviewer actually inspected and the limits of that medium. Model judgments of feel and long-term appeal remain provisional.
Report each assessment as verified (evidence and scope), unresolved (location and consequence), and not assessed (reason). Keep observed facts separate from subjective judgments. Aesthetic approval does not establish usability or correctness. Owner preferences calibrate character; retain their reasons and check task clarity too.
Use ui-quality-gate for operational inspection and corrections when installed. Otherwise, fix the most consequential observed gap and recheck it. If refinement stalls, revisit the cause. Keep remaining material gaps and evidence limits visible at delivery.

I. CONTRACT AND LEARNING
Use the project's existing design record for durable choices, reasons and scoped owner corrections. Point to implementation values in governing tokens and components. Create a record only when a decision needs to survive the session and the project lacks a suitable home. Generalize a lesson only with evidence across unlike products.

SPECIALIST OWNERSHIP
Vibe UI coordinates the relevant specialists within one direction. UI Quality Gate uses their evidence in the final inspection and reopens affected decisions.
